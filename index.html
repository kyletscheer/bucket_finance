💧 Bucket Finance Flow

Tell your money where to go, one bucket at a time

The Bucket System: Think of your accounts as buckets. Money flows from one to the next like a waterfall—fill bucket 1 to its goal, then overflow to bucket 2, and so on.
  • Order matters: Put essential accounts first (checking buffer, emergency fund), then debts by interest rate, then savings goals
  • Debts flow to $0: Use negative amounts for debts. Set goal to $0 and watch the red bar shrink as you pay it off
  • Monthly updates: Update your balances each month and move money as the tool suggests

Setup Wizard

Let's set up your buckets in the right order

Welcome to Bucket Finance! 💧

This system helps you prioritize where your money should go. We'll guide you through setting up your buckets in the optimal order based on financial best practices.

Key Principle: Money flows from one bucket to the next. Once bucket 1 reaches its goal, excess flows to bucket 2, and so on. This ensures you're always funding your priorities in the right order.

What you'll need:

  • Your current account balances
  • Knowledge of your monthly expenses
  • Information about any debts (balances and interest rates)
